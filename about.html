Who We Are

Texas-Based Commercial Masonry Contractor

C&S Masonry is a commercial masonry contractor based in Rockwall, TX. We work as a specialty subcontractor to general contractors and developers on commercial and multi-family projects across Dallas-Fort Worth and beyond. Our team handles brick, stone, stucco, EIFS, CMU, cast stone, and balustrade scopes.

Our crews are direct employees, not day labor. That means consistent quality, proper supervision, and accountability on every project.

C&S Masonry is a sister company of C&C Roofing. We operate under the same ownership and bring the same standards to exterior masonry and finish work.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every decision on the job site traces back to three commitments that the Cleveland family has held since day one.

◆

Workmanship

We hold every mortar joint, texture coat, and cast stone detail to a finish standard — not just a schedule. If it's going on a building, it has to be right.

◆

Direct Communication

When there's a problem on the job, you hear about it from us directly — not from an RFI log two weeks later. Surprises cost everyone money.

◆

On-Scope Delivery

We bid what we can build, submit on time, and close out clean. GCs and developers rely on us because we don't create problems at the end of the job.
