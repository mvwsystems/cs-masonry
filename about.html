Who We Are

A Rich History in Masonry Craftsmanship

C & S Brick, Stucco, and Stone was built on one core principle: deliver the quality you'd demand on your own building. The principals, Coy and Gene Cleveland, have spent their careers mastering every masonry trade — from three-coat stucco systems to ornamental cast stone detailing — and built C & S to carry that standard forward on every project.

Operating out of Rockwall, TX, we serve the full Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex and beyond, working primarily as a specialty subcontractor to commercial and multi-family GCs. Our crews are direct employees — not temp labor — which means our quality control starts at orientation, not the day a punch list comes out.

As the sister company to C&C Roofing, C & S benefits from a shared culture of accountability, direct communication, and a reputation that has been built over decades in the North Texas construction market.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every decision on the job site traces back to three commitments that the Cleveland family has held since day one.

◆

Craftsmanship First

We take pride in the finish, not just the schedule. Every mortar joint, every texture coat, every cast stone element is held to the standard of someone who will see it every day for decades.

◆

Direct Communication

When there's a problem on the job, you hear about it from us — not from an RFI log review two weeks later. Our superintendents communicate proactively with GC teams because surprises cost everyone money.

◆

Client-Focused Delivery

C & S has a rich history in the pride and craftsmanship it takes to please each client. We look forward to servicing each client's masonry needs with the same dedication we've brought to every job for over three decades.
