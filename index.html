Who We Are

Texas-Based Commercial Masonry

C&S Masonry is a commercial masonry contractor serving general contractors, developers, and property owners. Our team handles brick, stone, stucco, EIFS, CMU, cast stone, and balustrade scopes for commercial and multi-family projects across Texas.

A sister company of C&C Roofing, C&S Masonry brings the same commercial construction standards to masonry and exterior finish work.
