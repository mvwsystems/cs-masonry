Who We Are

Texas Masonry, Built on Craftsmanship

The principals of C & S bring over 70 combined years of masonry experience to every multi-family and commercial project. We specialize in all masonry veneer work — stucco, EIFS, brick, CMU, natural stone, manufactured stone, balustrades, and cast stone.

Based in Rockwall, TX, C & S is the sister company to C&C Roofing, operating with the same commitment to craftsmanship, client communication, and quality finishes that the Cleveland family has been known for across the DFW metroplex.

What We Do

Our Core
Services

From raw foundation to finished facade, C & S handles every masonry discipline with precision. Every trade performed in-house — no subcontracting your scope to unknown crews.

All Services
01
Exterior

Stucco & EIFS

Three-coat stucco systems and EIFS (Exterior Insulation and Finish System) for commercial facades requiring seamless, weatherproof, and highly aesthetic finishes.

02
Structural

Brick & Stone Veneer

Full brick and natural or manufactured stone veneer installation on multi-family and commercial structures. We handle everything from coursing layouts to final sealant.

03
Structural

CMU Block

Concrete Masonry Unit installation for load-bearing and non-load-bearing walls on commercial, retail, and multi-family projects throughout Texas.

04
Architectural

Cast Stone & Balustrades

Architectural cast stone elements and balustrade systems — columns, sills, copings, cornices, and ornamental features that elevate the finished look of any building.

05
Specialty

Natural Stone

Natural and manufactured stone cladding, accent walls, water features, and site elements. From limestone to travertine, we source and set to spec.

06
Multi-Family

Multi-Family Cladding

Complete exterior cladding packages for garden-style and mid-rise multi-family developments — coordinating all masonry scopes under one contract.
