What We Do

Our Services

Every masonry discipline — in-house, on-spec, on-schedule. From stucco to structural CMU, we cover the full scope of commercial masonry veneer.

01
Exterior Finish

Stucco & EIFS

Traditional three-coat stucco and Exterior Insulation and Finish Systems (EIFS) are the workhorses of modern commercial facades. C & S crews are trained in both systems and work with the major EIFS manufacturers to ensure full warranty compliance.

We handle lath and base coat prep, foam insulation board installation, base coat and mesh embed, finish coat texture, and final sealant — delivering a complete weatherproof, energy-efficient exterior envelope.

  • Three-coat stucco systems
  • EIFS — continuous insulation & finish
  • Manufacturer-approved applicators
  • Seamless transitions to adjacent cladding
  • Custom textures and color matching
02
Structural

Brick & Stone Veneer

Brick veneer and natural or manufactured stone veneer remain the gold standard for permanence and curb appeal on commercial and multi-family buildings. C & S has installed brick veneer on projects ranging from boutique medical offices to large-scale apartment communities.

Our estimating and field teams are experienced with running bond, stack bond, and herringbone patterns, and we work directly with suppliers to ensure unit color consistency across large facades.

  • Full brick veneer — all bond patterns
  • Natural stone veneer (limestone, granite, slate)
  • Manufactured stone systems
  • Mortar joint tooling and color match
  • Cavity wall and moisture barrier systems
03
Structural

CMU Block

Concrete Masonry Unit (CMU) construction forms the structural backbone of many commercial, retail, and multi-family buildings. Our crews lay standard, split-face, and architectural CMU — in load-bearing, non-load-bearing, and retaining wall applications.

We coordinate with structural engineers and GCs on reinforcing and grouting schedules to keep vertical construction on pace with envelope deadlines.

  • Standard, split-face, and architectural CMU
  • Load-bearing and non-load-bearing walls
  • Reinforcing and grouting in-house
  • Bond beam and lintel installation
  • Exterior and interior CMU packages
04
Architectural

Cast Stone & Balustrades

Architectural precast and cast stone details are what separate a good building from a great one. C & S installs balustrade systems, column capitals, sills, lintels, cornices, water tables, and custom ornamental elements crafted by our preferred cast stone fabricators.

We work with architects during design development to ensure details are buildable, affordable, and visually impactful on the finished structure.

  • Balustrade systems — full and half baluster
  • Column bases, capitals, and shaft wraps
  • Sills, lintels, copings, and keystones
  • Cornice and band course elements
  • Custom ornamental cast stone fabrication coordination
05
Specialty

Natural Stone

Nothing conveys permanence like natural stone. C & S installs limestone, travertine, granite, quartzite, and other natural stone materials on commercial building facades, feature walls, site walls, and landscape elements.

Our stone setters are experienced with both thin veneer and full-thickness natural stone, using appropriate setting methods for each substrate condition and project exposure.

  • Thin natural stone veneer
  • Full-thickness natural stone
  • Limestone, travertine, granite, quartzite
  • Site and landscape stone features
  • Interior accent stone applications
06
Multi-Family

Multi-Family Cladding Packages

Multi-family developers and GCs choose C & S because we can package the entire exterior cladding scope — stucco, EIFS, brick, stone, and cast stone — under a single subcontract. One point of contact. One schedule. One liability.

Our team has completed garden-style and wrap-style apartment projects across the DFW metroplex, working alongside top-tier GCs like Wood Partners, JPI, and Alliance Residential.

  • Combined stucco + brick + stone packages
  • Garden-style and mid-rise experience
  • Owner-direct and GC subcontract delivery
  • Phase-sequenced scheduling
  • Full closeout documentation and warranty
